# Third-party notices

## Material Design Icons (Pictogrammers)

The toolbar, title-bar and status-bar icons (`CleanPrint.App/Themes/Icons.xaml`) are paths taken from the
[Material Design Icons](https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/) collection, repository
<https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-SVG>.

The collection is released under the Pictogrammers Free License: the icons are licensed under the
**Apache License, Version 2.0** (<https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>). The paths were normalised to
WPF path syntax; the artwork itself is unmodified — with one exception: the Save-preset icon is the pack's `floppy` with a plus badge added
(a derivative; see `Scripts/make_save_icon.py`).

Icons used: folder-open, image, tune, cog, printer, export, chevron-left, chevron-right, page-first, page-last,
compare, rotate-left, rotate-right, arrow-expand-horizontal, arrow-expand-vertical, magnify-plus, magnify-minus,
vector-polygon, vector-rectangle, vector-ellipse, vector-circle-variant, vector-circle, vector-radius,
vector-bezier, format-text, trash-can-outline, floppy, undo, redo, content-cut, content-copy,
content-paste, select, cursor-move, format-align-left, format-align-center,
format-align-right, format-align-justify, reflect-horizontal, reflect-vertical, file-plus, plus-box-multiple, image-plus.
